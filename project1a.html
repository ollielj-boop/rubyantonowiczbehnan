Reignite Residency, Home Live Art

Reignite Residency with Home Live Art in Hastings, in collaboration with outside eye, Ed McKeon. 5 days, focused on investigating the use of our voices in composition, examining vocal techniques, sustaining single words to create harmony, and exploring phrases to build rhythm. Also examining spatial and immersive elements, including our movement and the positioning of the sound sources in relation to the audience. Culminated in a public sharing and feedback session
