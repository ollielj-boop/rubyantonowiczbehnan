La Cabine Residency, Cie Nathalie Beasse, Angers, FR

Over 2.5 weeks in France, we worked with voice and movement devising exercises. Through a series of workshops, we challenged our voices with extended vocal techniques and responded to this with our bodies to explore unusual sounds and surprising outcomes.

We tested actions, footsteps, and the sounds of our costumes so that our bodies became an active part of the performance's rhythm and structure. Along the way, we captured field recordings amongst the streets of Angers, responding to and weaving these sounds into the work.

The residency culminated in a live sharing with an audience from the theatre company, art collective and the local region.
